## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier corporate adventure aesthetic tailored for team building, leadership development, and experiential outdoor training across East Java. The visual tone bridges executive professionalism with natural exploration, avoiding the weathered rustic clichés of conventional outdoor gear in favor of an upscale, confident, and energetic presence.

The target audience encompasses HR directors, corporate event planners, enterprise leadership, and institutional teams seeking turnkey, safety-certified outbound programs. The emotional signature communicates unwavering reliability, dynamic teamwork, prestige, and open-air vitality.

The visual direction employs Modern High-Contrast with Warm Tactile Accents:
- Deep, tailored wine/burgundy tones provide authoritative anchoring reminiscent of executive retreats and refined gear.
- Warm sunlight gold introduces energetic illumination, focal points, and premium status markers.
- Bright, airy backgrounds ensure cognitive ease, maximum legibility, and an uplifting sense of expansive outdoor terrain.
- Precise geometric framing, rounded container cards, and delicate gold perimeter accents deliver an architectural, polished corporate polish.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to provide high visual impact while maintaining an uncluttered, executive aesthetic.

- **Primary (`#4A0E17`)**: Deep Maroon / Obsidian Wine. Replaces standard corporate blue or raw scarlet with an authoritative, grounded burgundy. Used for primary CTAs, master navigation surfaces, bold headline typography, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C8933E`)**: Radiant Warm Gold. Conveys peak performance, dawn sunlight, and tailored hospitality. Deployed strictly for interactive badges, key metrics, rating stars, active indicators, and micro-borders.
- **Neutral Core (`#1E1819`)**: Charcoal Espresso. A very dark, warm-tinted slate neutral for high-contrast, effortless body reading without the sterile harshness of pitch black.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#FBF8F6`)**: Primary content rests on crisp white surfaces framed by subtle warm alabaster canvasing (`#FBF8F6`), evoking natural stone and open skies while guaranteeing AAA contrast compliance.
- **Subtle Surface Container (`#F3ECE8`)**: Warm mist wash used for alternating section blocks, input field fills, and disabled container backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy partners **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, forward-moving display headers with **Inter** for neutral, hyper-legible body presentation.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Rendered in extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tightly controlled negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`). This establishes punchy, muscular titles that reflect athletic vigor and organizational leadership.
- **Body Text (Inter)**: Set with comfortable, open line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless scanning across long-form package itineraries, safety guidelines, and corporate proposal briefs.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used in uppercase tracking (`label-caps`) for category badges, location markers (e.g., "BATU, MALANG", "PACET, MOJOKERTO"), and package difficulty flags, creating instant navigational cues.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to communicate grandeur and structural organization:

- **Desktop (1200px and up)**: 12-column fluid grid, 24px (`1.5rem`) column gutters, max container constraint of 1280px, with generous 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas padding. Sections breathe with vertical block gaps of 80px to 120px to reflect vast outdoor topography.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px outer margins. Package comparison matrices and destination highlights collapse into 2-column alternating cards.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) screen margins. Horizontal swipe rails are utilized for outbound venues, photo galleries, and testimonial carousels to preserve vertical momentum.

Internal component rhythm follows a rigid 4px/8px incremental scale: 8px for badge padding, 16px for form control gutters, and 24px to 32px for container internal offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic grey drop shadows. Instead, it combines warm atmospheric tinting with razor-sharp micro-borders:

- **Resting Elevation (Base Layer)**: Soft ambient tinting using `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.04)` with a blur of 16px and vertical offset of 4px. All resting cards are encapsulated in a 1px border of `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.08)` or `#E8DFD8` to preserve crisp card geometry against pure white or `#FBF8F6` canvasing.
- **Interactive Hover State**: Cards lift dynamically via a 6px upward translation accompanied by a warm diffused shadow (`0 20px 32px -8px rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.12)`) and a transition of the hairline border to warm gold (`rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.45)`).
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Nav**: Navigation bars and quote calculators utilize a frosted, semi-translucent glass effect: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` backdrop fill with a 16px backdrop-filter blur, anchored by a bottom hairline stroke (`rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.06)`).
- **Featured Accent Highlight**: High-priority corporate retreat packages and primary badges receive a perimeter halo using a subtle 2px solid gold ring (`#C8933E`) paired with an inner 1px white buffer.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a balanced **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature language, blending contemporary product design with approachable, human-centered hospitality:

- **Cards & Content Modules**: 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`) corner radii, generating a soft yet structured rectangular framing that houses photography, program overviews, and client matrices securely.
- **Hero Banners & Feature Panels**: 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`) radius to set a soft, cinematic boundary for dynamic outdoor action shots (rafting, paintball, high ropes).
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) for high-action triggers, status badges, filter chips, and location tags. This distinct pill motif contrasts sharply against the structured card frames, driving higher click affordance.
- **Form Inputs & Interactive Fields**: 10px corner radius, balancing the ergonomic softness of the pill buttons with the architectural stability of the data cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep maroon background (`#4A0E17`), crisp white label (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 weight), pill shape, horizontal padding 28px, vertical padding 14px. On hover, background deepens to `#360A10` with a subtle warm gold glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Accent CTA**: Warm gold background (`#C8933E`), pure white or deep maroon text, pill shape. Used for priority contact actions such as "Konsultasi Paket Via WhatsApp" or "Request Proposal".
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1.5px solid border in `#4A0E17`, maroon label. On hover, surface fills with `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.05)`.

### Badges & Chips
- **Category / Location Badges**: Pill-shaped, subtle champagne-tinted surface (`rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.12)`), text rendered in `#A06E22` using `label-caps` (bold uppercase with +0.08em tracking).
- **Filter Chips**: 8px rounded or full pill, border 1px `rgba(74, 14, 23, 0.12)`, text in Charcoal Espresso. Active state swaps to solid Deep Maroon fill with crisp white text.

### Cards & Program Packages
- Multi-layered layout: 16px border-radius, pure white background, framed with a 1px border of `#EDE4DF`. 
- Top container features photography with a subtle gradient scrim (`rgba(30, 24, 25, 0.4)` to transparent).
- Bottom container contains duration pill tags, capacity indicators (e.g. "50 - 500 Pax"), bulleted team outcome metrics, and price anchoring highlighted in deep maroon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Surface: `#FBF8F6`, border: 1.5px solid `#E3D8D2`, 10px corner radius, padding: 14px 16px. 
- Focus state: Surface turns `#FFFFFF`, border color transitions smoothly to Deep Maroon (`#4A0E17`) with a 3px outer ring in `rgba(200, 147, 62, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 20px diameter, 1.5px border in `#C8933E`. 
- Selected state: Solid Deep Maroon (`#4A0E17`) fill with a pure white custom checkmark vector or center radio dot.

### Specialized Outdoor Components
- **Difficulty / Intensity Gauge**: 3-step segmented horizontal pill meter reflecting physical engagement levels (Light, Moderate, Dynamic) filled with Warm Gold (`#C8933E`).
- **Location Pin Header**: Icon badge combining a Warm Gold geo-pin with bold maroon destination titles (e.g., "Batu Highland Outbound Center").
